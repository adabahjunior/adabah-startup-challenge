EVALUATION MATRIX

Evaluation Pillars

A transparent evaluation framework prioritizing execution, problem importance, and numbers.

01 25% WEIGHT

Problem & Market Need

Painfulness and urgency of the problem, and clarity of the addressable customer segment.

02 25% WEIGHT

Solution & Innovation

Defensibility, technical execution, and tangible advantages over current alternatives.

03 25% WEIGHT

Traction & Feasibility

Demonstrated progress: prototype testing, customer interviews, active users, or sales.

04 25% WEIGHT

Pitch Clarity & Numbers

Founder communication, command of unit economics, and stage defense before the jury.
