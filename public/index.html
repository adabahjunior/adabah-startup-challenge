EVALUATION MATRIX

What the Judges Look For

A transparent, merit-driven evaluation framework prioritizing real-world execution and problem depth.

25% WEIGHT 🎯

Problem & Market Need

Is the problem painful, real, and well-understood? Is there a clearly addressable target audience?

25% WEIGHT ⚙️

Solution & Innovation

Does the solution tangibly solve the problem better or faster than current alternatives?

25% WEIGHT 📈

Traction & Feasibility

What progress has been made? Prototype, customer interviews, active users, or initial sales?

25% WEIGHT 🎙️

Pitch Clarity & Numbers

How persuasively does the founder defend the unit economics and vision on the live stage?
