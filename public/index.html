Programme Overview

What is The ADABAH Startup Challenge?

The ADABAH Startup Challenge is a student-focused entrepreneurship programme that gives aspiring founders and student entrepreneurs the opportunity to develop their businesses, learn from experienced entrepreneurs, receive practical mentorship, and compete for a cash prize.

The programme will culminate in a live pitch competition where selected participants will present their ventures to a panel of judges, entrepreneurs and industry professionals.

This is not just about having a great idea.

It is about building something real, knowing your numbers, understanding your customers, and learning how to communicate why your business deserves to grow.

The Process

How It Works

01 — APPLY

Submit Your Venture

Submit your startup or business for consideration through the online application. Applications will be reviewed based on the strength and potential of each venture.

02 — GET SELECTED

Cohort Onboarding

A group of promising student entrepreneurs will be selected to participate in the programme. Selected participants will receive further information about the mentorship programme and final pitch.

03 — MENTORSHIP

3-Week Structured Curriculum

Participants will take part in a structured three-week programme designed to strengthen their ventures and prepare them for the final pitch. Sessions will cover:

Pitch Development How to communicate your idea clearly and convincingly.
Business & Branding How to position your venture and build a stronger brand.
Customers & Market Understanding who you serve and why they should choose you.
Revenue & Business Model Understanding how your business makes money and grows.
Finding Opportunities & Investors How entrepreneurs can position themselves for partnerships, funding and growth.
04 — THE FINAL PITCH

Live Stage Competition

After the mentorship programme, finalists will pitch their ventures live before a panel of judges. Participants will have a limited amount of time to present their businesses and defend their ideas before the judges. The final pitch will assess the strength, potential and viability of each venture.

The Climax

THIS IS WHERE IDEAS MEET THE REAL WORLD.

Finalists will be challenged to clearly communicate their problem, solution, target market, business model, competitive advantage, progress, growth potential, and why they deserve to win.

The judges will not only be looking for impressive presentations. They will be looking for businesses worth betting on.

Evaluation

Judging Criteria

Participants will be evaluated based on 8 key areas:

Problem

How important and clearly defined is the problem being addressed?

Solution

How effectively does the product or service solve the problem?

Market

Is there a clear and meaningful market for the venture?

Business Model

How does the business make money, and is the model sustainable?

Traction

What progress has the venture made so far?

Innovation

What makes the solution different or valuable?

Scalability

Does the venture have the potential to grow?

Pitch

How clearly and convincingly does the founder communicate the opportunity?

The Purpose

Why The ADABAH Startup Challenge?

University is not only a place to prepare for employment. It is also a place to build, experiment, create and start.

The ADABAH Startup Challenge exists to give student entrepreneurs a platform to take their ideas seriously, develop practical business skills and connect with people who can help them move forward.

We believe students should not have to wait until graduation to start building something meaningful.
