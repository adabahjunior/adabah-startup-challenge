Origins & Purpose

Why The ADABAH Challenge Was Born

Transforming Tertiary Campuses Into Innovation Engines

Michael Akrofi Junior Adabah is a Ghanaian entrepreneur, venture builder, and ecosystem advocate passionate about unlocking the immense economic potential of university students across West Africa. Having witnessed firsthand the ingenuity of student innovators who often lack the early seed funding, hands-on mentorship, and institutional credibility required to take their ideas to market, Michael initiated The ADABAH Startup Challenge. His mission is rooted in a fundamental conviction: universities must not simply be factories for traditional corporate employment, but living laboratories where high-velocity tech products, sustainable agricultural solutions, and decentralized healthcare innovations are tested, funded, and launched into scalable commercial enterprises.
